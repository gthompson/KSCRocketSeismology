# KSC StationXML rebuild 2016–2026: audit report

`03_stationxml/KSC.xml` was rebuilt from a corrected per-channel-epoch sheet,
`03_stationxml/ksc_stations_master_v3.xlsx`. The sheet is generated by `make_master_v3.py` from the
read-only master `ksc_stations_master_v2.xlsx` plus the audit decisions below, and from the 2026
Centaur response files, the 2026 SDS and the Box sheets. The StationXML is written by
`build_ksc_stationxml.py`. That builder stops on a Centaur row whose `vpp` is not 1 or 40, an RT130
row with no gain, a sensor with no sensitivity, or overlapping epochs. Flovopy's builder silently
turns any other `vpp` into 40.

Each channel carries a datalogger description that includes the input range or gain (for example
`Centaur, Sensor B, input range 1 Vpp (preamp x40)`), a sensor description with its V/Pa or V/(m/s),
serials where known, and comments for status, change, evidence and notes.

* Every changed epoch, with old and new counts per unit: `changed_epochs.csv`, written by `50_changed_epochs.py`.
* Raw-count launch peaks for 145 launches (2016–2022) and night-time ambient levels: `launch_peaks_counts_2016_2022.csv` and `ambient_counts.csv`, both from `10_measure_launch_peaks.py`.
* Gain tests: `20_gain_tests.py`, with output in `20_gain_tests.log` and in the CSVs `T1_*` to `T4_*`.

### Geophones (RT130 + L-22): gain 1 → 32 (×32)

* `duringPASSCAL/CONFIG/network2018222…2019183.par` and `parfile.txt` give gain 32 on every EH? channel. Only the superseded draft `network20181016.par` says ×1.
* Launch peaks at 5–20 Hz against the co-located Trillium BCHH1 DHZ (T1), 2018-08..2019-08: the median ratio is **47 at gain 1 and 1.47 at gain 32** (n = 68). By channel at gain 32 the ratios are 1.36–1.48 for BHP1/2/4/6/7/8, 1.9 for BHP3 and 1.5–1.9 for BHP5.20.
* The leftover factor of ≈ 1.45 is the same on every geophone, so it is not a gain setting. It could be the L-22D generator constant: the NRL 62.95 V/(m/s) assumes the 10.8 kΩ shunt, while an unshunted 2200 Ω coil gives ≈ 88 V/(m/s), which would make the ratio ≈ 1.05. It could also be shallow installation against a posthole. **Not applied**; this is a question for the user.

### 2016 beach house: low-gain infraBSU 295

* `sitedb.calibration` puts sensor 295 on HD1 until 2016-03-04 and on HD2 afterwards, with calib 1.45 Pa/count against 0.12 for sensor 283.
* The swap happened during a service visit at **2016-03-04 ≈ 20:00 UTC**. There is a data gap from 19:58 to 20:05. Before it DD1/DD3 ≈ 0.07; after it DD2/DD3 ≈ 0.06 (hourly and 3-minute RMS). That is before SES-9 (23:35).
* Launch peaks (T2): DD1/DD2 and DD3/DD2 have a median of **14.7** over 6 launches, 2016-03-04..09-08, including AMOS-6. The pre-swap static fire on 2016-02-29 gives DD3/DD1 = 15.4.
* Applied: the sensor stage gain is ×1/14.7 on BCHH 00 DD1 (02-24..03-04T20:00), BCHH 00 DD2 (03-04T20:00..04-09) and BCHH 10 DD2 (05-17..09-09). The DD1/DD2 epochs are split at the swap.

### Chaparral M25 (new channels: BCHH1 DD1, BCHH3 HDF, BCHH4 HDF, 2026 B20/C384 DDF)

* These channels were missing from KSC.xml.
* Measured against co-located infraBSU on unclipped launches (T3, below ~60 Pa): 0.37–0.45 V/Pa at BCHH1 and 0.26–0.29 at BCHH4. Bigger launches saturate the M25's ±18 V output. **0.4 V/Pa** (the low-gain nominal) is used.
* **flovopy `build.py` and librockets.py use 0.05 V/Pa, which is 8× too low.**
* At 1 Vpp the M25 clips above ≈ 1.2 Pa, so every BCHH3 launch is clipped. At 40 Vpp it clips above ≈ 45 Pa.
* **SON1 DD1 (2017) is an infraBSU, not the Chaparral.** As infraBSU at 40 Vpp it reads 152 Pa for TDRS-M (SON1 DD3: 178 Pa); as a Chaparral it would read 0.017 Pa.

### Epochs and data coverage

* **2016 BCHH**: the SDS uses location codes 00 and 10 as KSC.xml does. The off-dates in the master are inclusive days, so epochs whose SDS has data on the off-date now end at the following midnight (FIRE, BCHH1, BCHH2, …).
* **BCHH1 DH?**: the 2019-02-04..2022-01-01 epoch (moved seismometer) was missing from KSC.xml and has been added.
* **BHP8**: end moved from 2019-02-05 to 2019-02-13 (data to 02-12; notes say "removed 2019-02-12?"). The SDS BHP8 data from 2019-04-10 to 06-25 is DAS 9BC5 after it moved to BHP7 (BHP7.10 covers the same days), so it gets no epoch.
* **DVEL (2017)**: excluded. There is no SDS data for 2017-02-13..2018-12-31 (none in `unmatched/` either), the vpp is unknowable, and the end date overlapped DVEL1–3.
* **DVEL3 2020-11-05..12-11** (data beyond the master epoch): Trillium epoch added at 40 Vpp; it recorded the 2020-12-11 Delta IV (SNR 12). Infrasound excluded: SNR ≈ 1 on 5 launches that BCHH2 recorded.
* **DVEL1 DD4–6 / HD4–6** (Sensor B port): excluded. They sit at the digitizer floor, with no launch signal and no evidence of their setting.
* **Raspberry Shakes**: R37BE extended to 2019-05-21..2021-05-20 and RCD29 to 2019-08-28..2021-05-20, where the SDS has data. Responses are copied from KSC.xml.
* **XA.MTEGL**: excluded. The Silicon Audio response is unknown, and the SDS channels are CN?.01, not EN?.
* **DG (2026 Certimus/Silicon Audio, MSFC)**: not included.
* **Remaining uncovered SDS days**: pre-deployment test days (BCHH3 2022-03-24, S39A3 03-25, TANK 02-24) and the exclusions above.

### 2026 network (new)

* **Centaurs**: B01, B03, B05, B14, B20, B24, B29, plus the January huddle under their serial names C202, C286, C371, C378, C383, C384, C386.
  * An epoch starts at every configuration change in the Centaur-written files: position, sample rate (B20 runs at 100 sps from 2026-04-04 23:36 to 06-14 15:33) or preamp.
  * The renames follow the file times: C202→B29 on 01-11, C371→B03 on 01-09, C386→B05 on 01-09, C384→B20 on 01-07.
  * The first epoch is extended back to the first SDS day, and the last ends at the last SDS day.
  * C286 and C383 have no response file, so they get the nominal configuration (identical in every file) at the huddle site.
* **Gems** (HD?, 2026-03-10..04-08): gemlog high-gain response, serials from `gem_mapping_artemis2_example.csv`, coordinates from `gem_gps_data`. **Gem 363 = `1R.UNK..HD1`**, unassigned, placed at the B20 HDF Gem position (≈ 8 m away by its GPS log).
* **SmartSolos** (DP?, 500 sps): 5 Hz, h = 0.7, 76.7 V/(m/s), 1 count = 1 mV (harvested in mV). **DPZ dip is +90 (reversed polarity).** The `SDS2026_metadata` sheet has no SmartSolo rows, so their dates come from the SDS (2026-03-10/11 to 04-04/05), with serials from `smartsolo_mapping_artemis2.csv`.

## Open questions for the user

1. **L-22 constant.** Is it 62.95 (NRL, shunted) or ≈ 88 V/(m/s) (no shunt)? The geophones read 1.45× the Trillium at gain 32.
2. **BCHH1 in 2020.** Is the ≈ 70× drop of the Trillium (and the low DD2) a known hardware fault? The data are flagged, not corrected.
3. **B20 and other 2026 stations after the 2026-09-22 service.** Only B20's 40 Vpp change is known. The other 2026 epochs end at the last downloaded day.
4. **flovopy.** `stationmetadata/build.py` uses 0.05 V/Pa for the Chaparral (should be 0.4) and maps a blank vpp to 40. Not edited.
