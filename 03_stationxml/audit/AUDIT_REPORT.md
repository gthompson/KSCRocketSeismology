# KSC StationXML rebuild 2016–2026: audit report

`03_stationxml/KSC.xml` was rebuilt from a corrected per-channel-epoch sheet,
`03_stationxml/ksc_stations_master_v3.xlsx`. The sheet is generated by `make_master_v3.py` from the
read-only master `ksc_stations_master_v2.xlsx` plus the audit decisions below, and from the 2026
Centaur response files, the 2026 SDS and the Box sheets. The StationXML is written by
`build_ksc_stationxml.py`. That builder stops on a Centaur row whose `vpp` is not 1 or 40, an RT130
row with no gain, a sensor with no sensitivity, or overlapping epochs. Flovopy's builder silently
turns any other `vpp` into 40.

Each channel carries a datalogger description that includes the input range or gain (for example
`Centaur, Sensor B, input range 1 Vpp (preamp x40)`), a sensor description with its V/Pa or V/(m/s),
serials where known, and comments for status, change, evidence and notes.

* Every changed epoch, with old and new counts per unit: `changed_epochs.csv`, written by `50_changed_epochs.py`.
* Raw-count launch peaks for 145 launches (2016–2022) and night-time ambient levels: `launch_peaks_counts_2016_2022.csv` and `ambient_counts.csv`, both from `10_measure_launch_peaks.py`.
* Gain tests: `20_gain_tests.py`, with output in `20_gain_tests.log` and in the CSVs `T1_*` to `T4_*`.

### Geophones (RT130 + L-22): gain 1 → 32 (×32)

* `duringPASSCAL/CONFIG/network2018222…2019183.par` and `parfile.txt` give gain 32 on every EH? channel. Only the superseded draft `network20181016.par` says ×1.
* Launch peaks at 5–20 Hz against the co-located Trillium BCHH1 DHZ (T1), 2018-08..2019-08: the median ratio is **47 at gain 1 and 1.47 at gain 32** (n = 68). By channel at gain 32 the ratios are 1.36–1.48 for BHP1/2/4/6/7/8, 1.9 for BHP3 and 1.5–1.9 for BHP5.20.
* The leftover factor of ≈ 1.45 is the same on every geophone, so it is not a gain setting. It could be the L-22D generator constant: the NRL 62.95 V/(m/s) assumes the 10.8 kΩ shunt, while an unshunted 2200 Ω coil gives ≈ 88 V/(m/s), which would make the ratio ≈ 1.05. It could also be shallow installation against a posthole. **Applied 2026-11-25 at the user's decision: 88 V/(m/s).** With RT130 gain 32, geophone sensitivities are now ×44.73 the old KSC.xml (32 × 88/62.95). The poles and damping are still the NRL shunted ones.

## SEED band codes (2026-11-26)

The Centaur names its channels with band code **D** at 250/500 sps, but D is for sensors with a corner period below 10 s. The Trillium Compact (120 s), the infraBSU (−3 dB at ≈ 0.035 Hz, ≈ 28 s) and the Chaparral M25 are broadband, so the SEED codes are **C** at 250–999 sps and **H** at 80–249 sps (user decision).
* `KSC.xml` and `KSC_1R_2016-2022.xml` now use C?? (265 channel epochs) and H?? (B20 2026-04-04..06-14 and the B24 huddle 2026-01-08, which ran at 100 sps under the D names). SmartSolo DP? (5 Hz geophone) stays D.
* The archives still use D??. Until `archive_fix/rename_band_codes.py` has been run on them, use **`KSC_sds_codes.xml`** (same responses, archive codes) with the data; the master sheet keeps the archive code in `sds_channel`.
* `rename_band_codes.py` changes only the 3-byte channel field of each record (lossless; tested sample-for-sample) and routes records by their own sample rate, so B20's mixed 500/100 sps day files split into C?? and H?? files.
* Code that hard-codes D[DH]? (ksclib `DEFAULT_CHANNEL_GROUPS`, `infrabsu_channels`, `ensemble.yaml`, `artemis2.yaml`, `restore_B29_DHZ.py`, notebooks, catalog seed-id lists) must change when the archives are renamed. New Centaur downloads will keep arriving as D?? unless the ingest renames them.
