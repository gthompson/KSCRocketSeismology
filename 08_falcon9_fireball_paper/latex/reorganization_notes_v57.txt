Falcon 9 manuscript reorganization: main57.tex / supplement56.tex

PURPOSE
-------
The manuscript has been reorganized from a conventional long Methods -> long
Results sequence into topic-based analysis sections. Within each scientific
question, the minimum method needed to understand the analysis is followed
immediately by the corresponding measurements/results. Interpretation remains
in a separate Discussion.

MAIN57 STRUCTURE
----------------
1. Introduction
2. Data and general processing
   - BCHH array and geometry
   - Calibration and waveform preparation
   - Video, meteorological, and regional data
   - Analysis workflow and timing coordinates
3. Forensic chronology and precursor search
4. Opening sequence and propagation timing
   - Close-range acoustic reference
   - Independent video and BCHH event timing
   - Weak-shock timing sensitivity
5. Event catalogue and temporal evolution
6. Waveform morphology and array propagation
7. Seismic/acoustic coupling
8. Acoustic energetics
9. Regional detectability and atmospheric propagation
10. Discussion
    - Forensic chronology and the precursor question
    - Differential timing and weak-shock propagation
    - Seismic/acoustic coupling and energy partition
    - Regional propagation and non-detection
    - Monitoring implications and limitations
11. Conclusions

EDITORIAL CHANGES
-----------------
- Removed the top-level Methods and Results sections.
- Moved method paragraphs next to the results they support.
- Kept Discussion separate so observation/measurement remains distinct from
  interpretation.
- Put the precursor search before the opening-event timing analysis.
- Put the same-camera Pad 39A timing method immediately before the observed
  DD2/DD3 differential timing results.
- Put the weak-shock sensitivity calculation immediately after the timing
  measurements, while retaining the broader physical interpretation in
  Discussion.
- Moved catalogue association/QC immediately before the 153-event catalogue
  and temporal-evolution results.
- Put array-processing definitions immediately beside the waveform and local
  array-propagation results.
- Put pressure-PGV and H1 definitions immediately beside their regression and
  transfer-function results.
- Separated acoustic energetics from regional screening in the main paper.
- Split the old supplementary S6 into a dedicated coupling section and a
  dedicated named-event/weak-shock/energetics section.
- Split regional interpretation into its own Discussion subsection.

CONTENT PRESERVATION
--------------------

- All supplementary figures were retained.
- The New Glenn energy-partition comparison remains in the Discussion and in
  detailed form in Supplementary Section S7.
- The pressure-PGV coupling and H1 transfer-function material is retained in

  reorganization.

FIGURE PATHS
------------
All \\includegraphics commands now use figure basenames only. The existing
\\graphicspath declarations provide the search directories. Explicit paths are
retained only in \\IfFileExists checks, where they are useful for testing
whether generated products exist.
