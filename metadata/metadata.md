### B20 Centaur Sensor B input range

B20 Sensor B records the infrasound channels, including the Chaparral M25
(DDF) and infraBSU channels DD1/DD2. These channels share the Centaur
input-range setting.

On 2026-09-23, the Sensor B input range was directly observed in the field
to be configured at 1 V peak-to-peak. It was changed during that visit to
40 V peak-to-peak to preserve the full dynamic range of the Chaparral M25,
which requires an input range of at least approximately 36 V peak-to-peak.

Thus:

- before 2026-09-23: B20 Sensor B = 1 V p-p
- from 2026-09-23: B20 Sensor B = 40 V p-p

The precise time of the 2026-09-23 configuration change should be added if
available.

Paul and Aiden visited B20 in mid-June 2026. It is possible that the input
range was changed during that visit and subsequently changed back, but
there is currently no evidence that this occurred. The 1 V p-p setting is
therefore the working configuration for the 2026-05-28 New Glenn event.

For an infraBSU 0.5-inch sensor, the corresponding overall sensitivities are:

- Centaur 1 V p-p: 736 counts/Pa
- Centaur 40 V p-p: 18.4 counts/Pa
